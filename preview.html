Features Implemented

How to Use

  • Click "Open Settings" to access the settings panel
  • Enable "Bearbeitungsmodus" (Edit Mode) to drag elements
  • Drag any HUD element to your preferred position
  • Your positions are automatically saved
  • Use "Reset Demo" to restore default values
